Flood-Net is not a flood warning service. It does not issue warnings, forecasts or public alerts, and nothing on it is an official statement about flooding, river conditions or public safety. Readings shown here can be late, wrong or missing. Never use Flood-Net to decide whether you or anybody else is safe.

For official warnings, go to the Bureau of Meteorology and your state's emergency service. In an emergency, call 000; for flood or storm help from the SES, call 132 500.

The agent API

The read-only API at /api/v1 and /api/mcp is open for scripts and AI assistants to read public station data, under the rate limits described in its documentation. Identify your client as that page asks, keep within the limits, and don't present what it returns as an official warning or forecast. We may block a client or address that misuses it.

Ask Claude

Station Health's Ask Claude briefing uses an AI model from Anthropic. Its answers are generated, can be wrong, and are a starting point for an engineer's judgement rather than a substitute for it. Use on Flood-Net's key is for approved editors and is limited per person and per day. If you use your own Anthropic key instead, that use is between you and Anthropic, under their terms and at your cost.
