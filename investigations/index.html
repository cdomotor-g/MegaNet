02 · 29 October 2026 · Toowoomba AL · Open: Toowoomba implicated, site fault not yet found

Toowoomba's quiet week: what changed when its repeater went off air

Toowoomba AL's repeater stopped transmitting at 04:35 on 25 October, 78 minutes after its rain counter reset to 0. On its channel, 151.525 MHz, corrupted copies fell from 53 to 19 per 1,000 at that moment, on the same traffic, back to the early-October level. Neither our receiver's update the day before nor anything else at our end caused it. Toowoomba's own carrier had been sending corrupted copies twice as often as the other repeaters on its channel, and it looks to have been failing since 15 October.

01 · 27 October 2026 · Billaboo AL and Blackdown Tableland AL · Open: cause narrowed, not confirmed

Climbing red crosses: corrupted relay copies at Billaboo and Blackdown Tableland

The crosses climbing above the rainfall line are second copies of real reports, damaged as they were relayed. Each arrives about a second after a clean copy, with its upper bits changed. Old ALERT frames have no checksum, and their check bits can't see damage in the last byte, so it gets through and reads high. The same fault explains Blackdown Tableland's odd-looking reset, and Emerald Radar is the only repeater both stations share.
